bf16 NN coop tile A/B at small M — M5 Pro, 2026-09-04
=====================================================

Question: `nn_coop_kernel` chose the 64x64 sg4 tile only for N <= 512 and
ignored M; no NN shape below M = 512 had ever been measured (audit item G4).

Method: paired, order-balanced, in-process A/B through the public `gemm`
(bf16 operands from `cast_f32_to_bf16`, f32 output, GemmBackend::TensorOps)
with a temporary env override selecting the tile. Twenty GEMMs per command
buffer (async encode, auto barriers) so the clock sees the kernel, eight
calls per arm per round (per-call median), six rounds alternating arm order,
median of rounds. Machine idle otherwise. Absolute times swing with thermal
load; only the paired ratio is the finding.

ratio = 128x64 time / 64x64 time (> 1: the 64x64 tile is faster)

Reading: at M <= 64 the 64x64 tile wins at every N and K (1.35-1.74x): a
128-row tile is at least half padding there. With full tiles the outcome
follows grid fill: the 64x64 tile wins while fewer than 64 default tiles
cover C (M = 128 at N <= 3072; M <= 512 at N = 768) and loses from 64 tiles
up (0.82x at M >= 256, N = K = 4096). Selection landed as
`n <= 512 || m <= 64 || ceil(m/128) * ceil(n/64) < 64`, pinned by
`nn_coop_kernel_selects_the_narrow_tile_by_grid_fill` in src/gemm.rs.
