SUMMARY
  * Head dim 64 (the vision tower's 12 heads x 64, 1 and 4 images of 2520 patches) and head dim 32 (BERT-small)
    now run the TensorOps FlashAttention-2 body (kernels/attn_tiled.h, ENCODER mode) instead of the scalar
    rows kernel: 2.79x and 2.91x at D=64 (1 and 4 images; median of per-round ratios, ranges [2.72, 2.86] and
    [2.66, 3.04]), about 2.3x at D=32 (median 2.30x, one contended round pulls the low end to 0.74).
    Verified. Geometry per head dim from the sweep below: D=64 BQ=32 BK=32 NSG=2, D=32 BQ=16 BK=32 NSG=1.
  * bf16 operand tiles (Q, K, V and the probability tile P bf16; scores, softmax, accumulator, output f32) run
    the f32 tiled kernel's shapes 3.0 to 3.8x faster at the text encoder's head dims (D=256 sliding layers
    3.03x and 3.06x at 8x1024 and 2x4096, D=512 global layers 3.62x and 3.74x) and 2.5x to 2.6x at the vision
    shapes, 17-19 TFLOP/s against 4.7-6.0 on the text shapes, 12-13 against 4.6-5.1 at D=64. Two independent
    repeats agree to 4 % at every shape (table below). Verified.
  * Against the scalar rows kernel the bf16 kernel is 7.1x (1 image) and 7.4x (4 images) faster at D=64.
    Verified.
  * End to end (bench_paired, base and new bench_embedgemma2 interleaved, 6 rounds, BENCH_ITERS=6): the bf16
    forward takes 0.867x, 0.662x and 0.535x of the time at 32x256, 8x1024, 2x4096 (198.0 -> 171.6, 301.2 -> 197.6,
    452.8 -> 244.2 ms); the exact-f32 forward 0.995x, 0.991x, 0.997x (every range straddles 1: unchanged within
    noise, as the digests below require). Verified.
  * Accuracy: the bf16 kernel is held to a bound derived from bf16's roundoff before the first run
    (tests/embedgemma2_kernels.rs, section 2b); every element of 7 cases (D=64/256/512, 2520-row vision shape
    with a padding suffix, windows 1 to 512, a length-0 row, device lengths past T) sat at 0.04-0.40 of its
    6-sigma bound against the exact-input f64 reference and 0.29-0.47 against the bf16-rounded-input one.
    The real checkpoint's bf16 forward stays inside its unchanged bounds: cos vs fp32 0.99999337-0.99999808
    on all 8 texts, mean 1-cos 4.9e-6 (4.1e-6 before; transformers' bf16: 4.7e-5). Verified.
  * The f32 kernels are unchanged: ExactF32 embeddings and layer traces hash identically (tiny model and the
    real checkpoint, FNV-1a over every f32 bit) before and after, and the LLVM IR of the Qwen3.5 tiled body
    (qwen35_attn_tiled_h256_q32_k32_sg4) and of qwen35_attn_qk_norm_rope's float path is identical after
    normalizing names and ids. Verified (section "ExactF32 and the shared template").

MACHINE STATE AND NOISE
  The GPU was shared. During the sweeps other sessions were running: tessl lane tests and benches in sibling
  worktrees, three concurrent scripts/hosted_tests.sh runs, a Lappi tools/mac_heavy.sh job, GitPulse cargo
  builds and test runs (CPU-heavy, 340 % on one rustc at one point). GPU utilization was not sampled (ioreg was
  not queried), so the contention is known only from its effect: an arm's self% below is how far its own
  per-round medians moved across rounds (max/min - 1), and it ranged from 4 % to 360 % between runs. The run of
  the text-shape sweep is the noisiest (self 25-270 %, other lanes' GPU tests landing in it); the production
  repeats (prod_a, prod_b) ran in a quieter window (self 4-31 % at the text shapes) and are the numbers to cite.
  Every run exits 3 by design: bench_encoder_attn refuses to call a run clean when the baseline arm moved more
  than 10 % across rounds, and that tripped in every run (listed at the end of each raw output), so a ratio
  here is a median of per-round ratios with its lo/hi range, never a single timing, and a ratio whose range
  straddles 1.0 is not a result. Treat any min above as good to about +-15 %, and any difference between two
  geometries under 15 % as noise.

METHOD
  Operands: Q, K uniform [-0.25, 0.25), V uniform [-1, 1); lens = T (no padding); K/V capacity = T. A sample is
  BENCH_REPS=2 back-to-back dispatches queued asynchronously and one synchronize, divided by 2; a round is
  BENCH_ITERS=3 samples whose median is the round's figure; BENCH_ROUNDS rounds (16 for the sweeps, 24 for the
  production repeats, 40 for D=32). Arms of a shape alternate forward on even rounds and reversed on odd
  (common::run_paired). "min ms" is the minimum over rounds of the round figure (min-of-N), "med ms" the median,
  "vs base arm" the median over rounds of base/arm (>1: faster than the base) with the lowest and highest round.
  Before a sweep candidate is timed its output is compared with the rows kernel's (f32 operands within 1e-4 of
  max, bf16 within 5e-2); none was rejected. TFLOP/s counts 4*D flops per unmasked (q, k) pair per head per
  sequence (window and lens as the kernels apply them).
  Shapes: text_swa = 4 query heads over 2 KV heads of 256, window 512; text_glob = 4 over 1 KV head of 512, every
  key; vision = 12 heads of 64, MHA, every key, 2520 patches; bert32 = 12 heads of 32, 8 x 512.

ACCURACY OF THE bf16 KERNEL (tests/embedgemma2_kernels.rs, section 2b; bound fixed before the first run)
  u = 2^-8. The first-order error of one output element has four independent sources (V rounding; P rounding
  with the row sum over the rounded weights; K rounding; Q rounding, bounded by Cauchy-Schwarz); the test
  computes each element's sigma_x in f64 from the exact inputs and holds the kernel to 6 sigma_x + 2e-5 max|ref|
  (the f32 kernels' own bound), and, against the f64 reference of the bf16-ROUNDED inputs (removes V, K, Q
  rounding), to 6 sigma_p + 2e-5 max|ref|. Observed (max over the case; fraction = worst element's error over
  its bound):
