EmbeddingGemma 2 audio tower: per-stage profile, before and after (Apple M5 Pro, real checkpoint 914f7f89)

before = a4eb1b8 (df5d329's kernels plus the stage hook; 26 stages, 343 dispatches per 1-clip forward set)
after  = the lane's head (19 stages, 210 dispatches), PROFILE_ROUNDS=7 for after, 5 for before
method = profile_embedgemma2_audio: stage-mask variants only/skip/none/full/encode on zeroed activations, interleaved
         rounds, min over rounds, harness floor removed. "alone" is the stage run alone; "marg" is full minus skip.

HOW TO READ THIS FILE. The GPU was shared with other jobs while this ran (gpu util 90 % before; 93-100 % after;
the `round spread` printed per workload is max/min over rounds minus 1). The before profile ran at 5-16 % spread, the
after profile at 36-176 %. The GEMM kernels are the same before and after (q, k, v are one 1024 -> 3072 GEMM
instead of three 1024 -> 1024), so on a shared GPU their time is the in-run control: GEMM stages alone were 12.6 / 14.6 / 568.6 ms before and 19.2 / 19.2 / 519.9 ms after for the
three workloads. The two small clips were therefore 1.3-1.5x slowed by load in the after run; the batch was not
(its GEMMs are 9 % faster, which is also load, in the other direction). Use the table below (other / GEMM, a ratio
taken inside one run) and the interleaved benchmark (embedgemma2_audio_speed_m5pro.txt), not the absolute
milliseconds across the two runs. The stage ranking inside a run is what the profile is for.
