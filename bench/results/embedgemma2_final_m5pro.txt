  * ExactF32 (the default) is 1.66x faster at 1x16 (lane G's opt-in split-K for few-tile GEMMs)
    and unchanged elsewhere: every other ratio's range contains 1.0 or sits within 2 % of it. Its
    GEMMs are compute-bound on the exact-f32 matrix rate (lane D), which no lane changed.
  * Bf16 is 2.75x to 3.0x faster than 60a6240 from 64x32 up and 1.89x at 1x16 (lane B's bf16
    GEMMs, lane E's bf16 attention tiles). Accuracy: cosine 0.99999 or better against fp32 on the
    real checkpoint, mean 1-cos about 10x smaller than transformers' own bf16 forward
    (tests/embedgemma2_model.rs).
  * Device peak above the model is 3.0x to 3.9x lower in both modes (lane H: activations released
    before the wait, a 16 MiB per-layer-input run, q|k|v sharing gate|up's buffer). The timed
    workloads run back to back in one process, so the before figures include the second activation
    set that 60a6240 kept across consecutive encodes (lane D's accounting, reconciled to 0.01 MiB in
    embedgemma2_stage_profile_m5pro.txt).
