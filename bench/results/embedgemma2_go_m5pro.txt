  paired us    Go wall time minus the engine's own timing of the same call, per call, minimum:

               The figure to read. The medians in the .json are inflated several-fold by the load.
  go-direct us difference of the minima of two processes: it also carries the difference between
               two processes' GPU times, so it is noise of about +-100 us here and is negative once.
               Do not read it as the cost.
  main us      tessl's C ABI called on the process's main thread. The "direct" column is the same
               on a thread of its own with gusset's 8 MiB worker stack, which is what the Go engine's
