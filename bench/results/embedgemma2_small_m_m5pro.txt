EmbedGemma2 small-M GEMMs (lane G): an opt-in parallel K-partition path for the few-tile exact-f32 NN GEMMs
===========================================================================================================
Machine: Apple M5 Pro, 64 GB, macOS 27, 2026-10-15. Before = eg2/integrate 27a0407 built in its own
worktree and target dir; after = the same tree plus this change (branch worktree-agent-a17ffa3c9da42a5f0).
Data: embedgemma2_small_m_m5pro.json (every round of every lane, the merged statistics, the per-GEMM rows).
Plot: embedgemma2_small_m_m5pro.png (embedgemma2_small_m_plot.py). Merge: `results_report small-m` (src/bin/results_report).
Tools: target/release/bench_paired over bench_embedgemma2 (whole encode); bench_gemm_routes lane nnsmall
(one GEMM). EMBEDGEMMA2_SNAPSHOT = google/embeddinggemma-2 snapshot 914f7f89142e.

Verified = measured here and in the JSON. Inferred = follows from measured numbers by the argument given.
Unverified = not measured.

WHAT CHANGED
  An exact-f32 NN GEMM whose C is a few tiles over a long K ran one single-simdgroup threadgroup per 32x32
  tile, each walking all of K (down, o, ple at 16 rows: 16 threadgroups), so its time was that walk's
  latency, 17-22 % of the copy bandwidth. A call can now opt in (gemm_small_m(a, b, c, backend, scratch))
  to running every K-partition's tiles in one dispatch into a scratch slice per partition, with
  reduce_partitions_f32 adding the slices in order (the TN split-K's design):
  matmul2d_tensorops_nn_splitk_par_f32. Gate (src/gemm.rs nn_small_par_k_tile): N <= 512, K >= 512, under 64
  tiles of 32x32 (M <= 96 at N 512), about 128 threadgroups in all, the width sized from one tile row so it
  does not move with M. At the forward's shapes: down and o global (K 2048, N 512): 8 partitions of 256; o
  sliding (K 1024): 8 of 128; ple_gate / ple_out (K 512): 4 of 128, at every M up to 96. qkv (N 2048-3072) and
  gate|up (N 4096) keep the single dispatch.
  THE OPT-IN IS THE CALL'S. gemm, gemm_tiled, ExactF32.nn, the vision and audio towers (embedgemma2::project),
  Qwen3.5 and every external consumer keep exactly the route they had on 27a0407: no split-K, the same bits.
  Only EmbedGemma2Model's own project (the text encoder, ExactF32) calls gemm_small_m; Bf16 is untouched.
  The scratch is the forward's own idle activation buffer (acts.qkv, which holds nothing at the o, down and
  per-layer-input GEMMs; acts.attn at the qkv and gate|up GEMMs), so the path adds no device memory.
  Accuracy: only the summation order moves (K in partitions summed in order, deterministic); tests hold every
  result to gamma_(K+8) * sum|a.b| (the bound all exact-f32 GEMMs meet), worst 0.0039 of it. A text
  sequence's embedding alone (the lane, 10-40 rows) against the same one batched (200 rows, one dispatch) moves
  by 1.3e-7 at real GEMM shapes, 1.7e-7 on the real checkpoint, against the existing bound of 1e-5.

MACHINE STATE
  The GPU read 10 % utilization when the committed runs began and the control lane's median is 0.997-1.001 on
  every workload, with ranges of 0.94-1.04 (1x16) and under 0.04 elsewhere: a quiet machine, every round clean.

METHOD
  bench_paired, three lanes per run (before 27a0407, after, control = before again), a fresh process per
  lane per round, lane order reversed on odd rounds, BENCH_ITERS encodes per process and the process's
  min-of-N reported; per workload the figure is per round. Runs, all exact f32: 1x16 and 1x64, 40 rounds x 20
  iterations (warmup 5); 64x32, 30 rounds x 10 (warmup 2); 8x1024, 16 rounds x 3 (warmup 1). "clean rounds" are
  rounds in which the control lane is within 10 % of the before lane on that workload. The one-GEMM figures
  come from bench_gemm_routes lane nnsmall: the plain single dispatch (raw), production (gemm_small_m, a pool
  scratch), and forced widths, interleaved, 12 rounds x 8 calls x 24 GEMMs, weights cycled over 24 copies as
  in a forward, every arm's output checked against the plain one first (<= 2.2e-6 of the output's scale).

RESULTS
  Whole encode, after / before (< 1 is faster). "min of rounds" is the house min-of-N over rounds; "median
  ratio" the median over rounds of the per-round ratio with its lowest and highest round, and the control's
  same figures; "clean" the rounds the control agrees with the before lane.

READING THE RESULTS
  1x16 (the latency workload): 0.602x of the time (1.66x faster) with the control at 0.997, every one of 40
  rounds between 0.58 and 0.63; 1x64 0.698x. The stage profile's recommendation 3 estimated 7.3 -> 4.1 ms
  (1.8x) for 70 % of the copy ceiling on the four shapes; measured 7.1 -> 4.2 ms with 64 %. Inferred, not
  measured: that the rest is the per-GEMM reduce dispatch and barrier (a fused reduce, or the reduce folded
  into the following norm + residual kernel, would remove it) and the unchanged qkv / gate|up /
  PLE-projection stages.
  64x32 and 8x1024 do not regress: 1.000x and 1.000x, 30 and 16 clean rounds. Their GEMMs are past the gate
  (M = 2048 and 8192 rows; src/gemm.rs small_m_select tests assert the gate returns none there, and
  tests/embedgemma2_small_m.rs asserts the traced forward of 200 rows dispatches the lane zero times).

VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNVERIFIED
  Verified: the lane's results against an f64 reference at M in {1, 7, 16, 63, 64} and the real K / N, through
  gemm_small_m and the forced entry (tests/gemm_small_m.rs; mutation-checked in the earlier build: dropping the
  A partition offset, or skipping one tile of one partition, fails 4 of 7 tests with errors of 500-1000x the
  budget, and a poisoned scratch turns an unwritten element into a NaN); bit-for-bit repeats; the route per
  shape with and without the opt-in; a caller's scratch (used when big enough, the single dispatch when one
  element short, an error when bf16); the width not moving with M inside the gate; the encode speedups and
  per-GEMM ratios above; the contracts above.
  Inferred: that the 64x32 and 8x1024 kernels are the same as 27a0407's (from the gate's tests and the traced
  200-row forward, not from a dispatch trace of the benchmark's own 2048-row forward).
  Unverified: a stage profile of the new build; other consumers' (ojas, qd-metal, MLSystemsLab) behaviour is
  unchanged by construction (they call gemm, which is untouched; not run here). A bf16 lane (commit b85f5e1,
  1x16 0.79x) is not part of this change: it moved the bf16 text alone-vs-batched check by 2.4e-4 to 5.7e-4
