EmbedGemma2 small-M GEMMs (lane G): parallel K-partitions for the few-tile exact-f32 NN GEMMs
==============================================================================================
Machine: Apple M5 Pro, 64 GB, macOS 27, 2026-10-15. Before = eg2/integrate df5d329 built in its own
worktree and target dir; after = the same tree plus this change (branch worktree-agent-a17ffa3c9da42a5f0).
Data: embedgemma2_small_m_m5pro.json (every round of every lane, the merged statistics, the per-GEMM rows).
Plot: embedgemma2_small_m_m5pro.png (embedgemma2_small_m_plot.py). Merge: embedgemma2_small_m_report.py.
Tools: target/release/bench_paired over bench_embedgemma2 (whole encode); bench_gemm_routes lane nnsmall
(one GEMM). EMBEDGEMMA2_SNAPSHOT = google/embeddinggemma-2 snapshot 914f7f89142e.

Verified = measured here and in the JSON. Inferred = follows from measured numbers by the argument given.
Unverified = not measured.

WHAT CHANGED
  An exact-f32 NN GEMM whose C is a few tiles over a long K ran one single-simdgroup threadgroup per 32x32
  tile, each walking all of K (down, o, ple at 16 rows: 16 threadgroups), so its time was that walk's
  latency, 17-22 % of the copy bandwidth. It now runs every K-partition's tiles in one dispatch into a scratch
  slice per partition and reduce_partitions_f32 adds the slices in order (the TN split-K's design):
  matmul2d_tensorops_nn_splitk_par_f32. Gate (src/gemm.rs nn_small_par_k_tile): N <= 512, K >= 512, under 64
  tiles of 32x32 (M <= 96 at N 512), about 128 threadgroups in all, the width sized from one tile row so it does
  not move with M. At the forward's shapes: down and o global (K 2048, N 512): 8 partitions of 256; o sliding
  (K 1024): 8 of 128; ple_gate / ple_out (K 512): 4 of 128, at every M up to 96. qkv (N 2048-3072) and
  gate|up (N 4096) keep the single dispatch.
  Accuracy: only the summation order moves (K in partitions summed in order, deterministic); tests hold every
  result to gamma_(K+8) * sum|a.b| (the bound all exact-f32 GEMMs meet), worst 0.0039 of it. src/embedgemma2.rs
  is unchanged: project() goes through gemm.
  NOT DONE: a bf16 lane (GemmOperands::Bf16). It was built and measured (1x16 0.79x, down 1.1x, o 1.4-1.7x) and
  removed: its short sequences alone and in a batch differ by 2.4e-4 to 5.7e-4 in the real-checkpoint bf16 text
  test, against a bound of 1e-5 (BATCH_ABS), because a changed f32 summation order flips bf16 roundings. The
  work is in commit b85f5e1 on this branch.

MACHINE STATE
  The GPU was loaded by other sessions through most of the development and read 52 % utilization when the
  committed runs began; those runs are quiet (the 1x16 f32 encode reads 7.2 ms before, the committed stage
  profile's 7.3 ms; the control lane's median is 0.997-1.002 on every workload). An earlier set of runs under
  97-99 % utilization gave the same medians with ranges of 0.15-4.8 and is not committed.
  Single rounds still spike (1x16 ratio low 0.38, 8x1024 0.50 and 1.63): the control lane shows the same
  spikes in the same rounds (0.61 and 0.50), so they are the machine and not the change; the medians and the
  clean-round subsets below are the claim.

METHOD
  bench_paired, three lanes per run (before df5d329, after, control = before again), a fresh process per
  lane per round, lane order reversed on odd rounds, BENCH_ITERS encodes per process and the process's
  min-of-N reported; per workload the figure is per round. Runs, all exact f32: 1x16 and 1x64, 40 rounds x 20
  iterations (warmup 5); 64x32, 30 rounds x 10 (warmup 2); 8x1024, 16 rounds x 3 (warmup 1). "clean rounds" are
  rounds in which the control lane is within 10 % of the before lane on that workload. The one-GEMM figures
  come from bench_gemm_routes lane nnsmall: the plain single dispatch (raw), production (gemm), and forced
  widths, interleaved, 12 rounds x 8 calls x 24 GEMMs, weights cycled over 24 copies as in a forward, every
  arm's output checked against the plain one first (<= 2.2e-6 of the output's scale).

RESULTS
  Whole encode, after / before (< 1 is faster). "min of rounds" is the house min-of-N over rounds; "median
  ratio" the median over rounds of the per-round ratio with its lowest and highest round, and the control's
  same figures; "clean" the rounds the control agrees with the before lane.

READING THE RESULTS
  1x16 (the latency workload): 0.628x of the time (1.59x faster) with the control at 0.999; 1x64 0.727x. The
  stage profile's recommendation 3 estimated 7.3 -> 4.1 ms (1.8x) for 70 % of the copy ceiling on the four
  shapes; measured 7.2 -> 4.5 ms with 59 %. Inferred, not measured: that the rest is the per-GEMM reduce
  dispatch and barrier (a fused reduce, or the reduce folded into the following norm + residual kernel, would
  remove it) and the unchanged qkv / gate|up / PLE-projection stages.
  64x32 and 8x1024 do not regress: 1.001x and 0.996x, 30 and 15 clean rounds. Their GEMMs are past the gate
  (M = 2048 and 8192 rows; src/gemm.rs small_m_select tests assert the gate returns none there,
  tests/gemm_small_m.rs routing_by_shape asserts the traced kernel is the single dispatch).

REAL-CHECKPOINT TESTS (--ignored --test-threads=1, EMBEDGEMMA2_SNAPSHOT / _REF_DIR / _MM_REF_DIR)
  Green: embedgemma2_model (f32: texts alone vs batched 0 to 1.7e-7; bf16: 0 exactly, the bf16 path is
  unchanged), embedgemma2_e2e_model, embedgemma2_vision_model (images bit-identical alone vs batched),
  embedgemma2_mm_model (both tests; audio alone vs batched 0 and 1.6e-7 / 2.1e-7), and
  embedgemma2_audio_model's second test needs EMBEDGEMMA2_MM_REF_EAGER_DIR (not set, red on df5d329 too).
  RED: embedgemma2_audio_model audio_tower_matches_transformers_sdpa. Its alone-vs-batched check is bit
  equality of each clip's soft tokens (df5d329: bit-identical true); with the lane the soft tokens differ by
  7.6e-6 and 9.5e-6. Cause: the tower's soft-token projection is a GEMM of K 1536, N 512, so inside the gate:
  a clip alone is 25 or 83 rows (the lane), the two clips batched 108 rows (one dispatch), and the two sum K
  in different orders. Everything else in that test (every layer and the tower output against the
  transformers reference, 3.3e-7 to 1e-5 of scale) passes. No gate value fixes this: any M threshold has a
  short sequence alone below it and the same batched above it.

VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNVERIFIED
  Verified: the lane's results against an f64 reference at M in {1, 7, 16, 63, 64} and the real K / N, through
  gemm, ExactF32.nn and the forced entry (tests/gemm_small_m.rs; mutation-checked in the earlier build:
  dropping the A partition offset, or skipping one tile of one partition, fails 4 of 7 tests with errors of
  500-1000x the budget, and a poisoned scratch turns an unwritten element into a NaN); bit-for-bit repeats;
  the route per shape; the width not moving with M inside the gate; the whole non-ignored suite on the final
  code (1361 of 1362 pass; the one red is tests/capi.rs, which fails 2-3 of 21 in parallel on df5d329 too and passes 21/21 at --test-threads=1); the
  encode speedups and per-GEMM ratios above.
  Inferred: that the 64x32 and 8x1024 kernels are the same as df5d329's (from the gate's tests and the routing
  test, not from a dispatch trace of the benchmark's own forward).
  Unverified: a stage profile of the new build (the profiler's wait-for-quiet never saw a quiet window);
  other consumers' (ojas, qd-metal, MLSystemsLab) bit-level expectations at small-M f32 NN shapes. The bits
  differ from df5d329's there, inside the bound above, and a row's bits depend on M across the gate (M <= 96
  and M >= 97 at N 512 sum K in different orders; one dispatch computed a row the same at any M). rg over
  ojas, Lappi-decision, MLSystemsLab, sparsl found no golden that pins small-M GEMM bits, but qd-metal's
  tests/gpu.rs and ledger_rows.rs assert logits "bit identical" between two arms of a decision, which holds only
  while both arms take the same side of the gate at every GEMM; not run here.
  Not changed, and not a latency-bound shape: the NT per-layer-input projection (131-176 us, one dispatch per
  forward, 384 tiles).
