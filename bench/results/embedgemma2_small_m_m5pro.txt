EmbedGemma2 small-M GEMMs (lane G): parallel K-partitions for the few-tile NN GEMMs
====================================================================================
Machine: Apple M5 Pro, 64 GB, macOS 27, 2026-10-15. Before = eg2/integrate df5d329 built in its own
worktree and target dir; after = the same tree plus this change (branch worktree-agent-a17ffa3c9da42a5f0).
Data: embedgemma2_small_m_m5pro.json (every round of every lane, the merged statistics, the per-GEMM rows).
Plot: embedgemma2_small_m_m5pro.png (embedgemma2_small_m_plot.py). Merge: embedgemma2_small_m_report.py.
Tools: target/release/bench_paired over bench_embedgemma2 (whole encode); bench_gemm_routes lane nnsmall
(one GEMM). EMBEDGEMMA2_SNAPSHOT = google/embeddinggemma-2 snapshot 914f7f89142e.

Verified = measured here and in the JSON. Inferred = follows from measured numbers by the argument given.
Unverified = not measured.

WHAT CHANGED
  An NN GEMM whose C is a few tiles over a long K ran one single-simdgroup threadgroup per 32x32 tile, each
  walking all of K (down, o, ple at 16 rows: 16 threadgroups), so its time was that walk's latency, 17-22 % of
  the copy bandwidth. It now runs every K-partition's tiles in one dispatch into a scratch slice per partition
  and reduce_partitions_f32 adds the slices in order (the TN split-K's design). Exact f32:
  matmul2d_tensorops_nn_splitk_par_f32; bf16: matmul2d_tensorops_nn_splitk_par_bf16_f32. Gate
  (src/gemm.rs nn_small_par_k_tile): under 64 tiles of 32x32 (exact f32) or under 16 tiles of 64x64 (bf16), a
  K of two or more partitions of 128 or more, about 128 (exact f32) or 64 (bf16) threadgroups in all. At the
  forward's shapes: down and o global (K 2048, N 512): 8 partitions of 256 at M <= 32; o sliding (K 1024): 8
  of 128; ple_gate / ple_out (K 512): 4 of 128. qkv (64-96 tiles) and gate|up (128) keep the single dispatch.
  Accuracy: only the summation order moves (K in partitions summed in order, deterministic); tests hold every
  result to gamma_(K+8) * sum|a.b| (the bound all exact-f32 GEMMs meet), worst 0.0039 of it (exact f32), 0.0015
  (bf16). src/embedgemma2.rs is unchanged: project() goes through gemm / GemmOperands::Bf16.nn.

MACHINE STATE AND NOISE (the limit of every number below)
  Other sessions shared this GPU for the whole of the work: ioreg "Device Utilization %" read 0-100 % between
  and during runs (97-99 % at the start and end of the per-GEMM runs), from other worktrees' test and bench
  processes and from other users' sessions; the stage profiler's wait-for-quiet never saw a quiet window and
  was stopped, so no stage profile of the new build exists. Absolute times are therefore inflated and
  unstable: the 1x16 f32 encode, 7.3 ms in the committed stage profile, reads 9.4 ms at its best round here, and
  per-round ratios between lanes run from 0.15 to 4.8. Each paired run carries a control lane, the before binary run a
  second time, whose ratio to the first lane is what noise looks like. A delta inside the control's spread is
  noise; the statistics under each table say which.

METHOD
  bench_paired, three lanes per run (before df5d329, after, control = before again), a fresh process per
  lane per round, lane order reversed on odd rounds, BENCH_ITERS encodes per process and the process's
  min-of-N reported; per workload the figure is per round. Runs: 1x16 and 1x64, 40 rounds x 20 iterations
  (warmup 5); 64x32, 30 rounds x 10; 8x1024, 16 rounds x 3 (warmup 1); each in exact f32 (default) and with
  BENCH_PRECISION=bf16. "clean rounds" are rounds in which the control lane is within 10 % of the before lane
  on that workload. The one-GEMM figures come from bench_gemm_routes lane nnsmall: the plain single dispatch
  (raw), production (gemm), and forced widths, interleaved, 12 rounds x 8 calls x 24 GEMMs, weights cycled
  over 24 copies as in a forward, every arm's output checked against the plain one first (<= 2.1e-6 of the
  output's scale).

RESULTS
  Whole encode, after / before (< 1 is faster). "min of rounds" is the house min-of-N over rounds; "median
  ratio" the median over rounds of the per-round ratio with its lowest and highest round, and the control's
  same figures; "clean" the rounds the control agrees with the before lane.

READING THE RESULTS
  1x16 (the latency workload): exact f32 0.63x of the time in the 13 clean rounds (all-round median 0.669,
  min of rounds 0.716, i.e. 1.4-1.6x faster); bf16 0.79x (0.777, 0.711). 1x64: 0.81x / 0.85x. The control
  lane's median is 0.998-1.06 on the same rounds, its range as wide as the lane under test's, so the claim
  rests on the medians of 40 interleaved rounds and on the clean-round subsets agreeing with them, not on any
  one round.
  64x32 and 8x1024 must not regress: those workloads' GEMMs are all past the gate (M = 2048 and 8192 rows;
  src/gemm.rs small_m_select tests assert the gate returns none there, tests/gemm_small_m*.rs routing_by_shape
  asserts the traced kernel is the single dispatch), so the kernels dispatched are the ones df5d329 dispatches
  and the only code added on those paths is a gate call. Measured: medians 0.94-1.03 against a control of
  0.99-1.07 with ranges of 0.3-4.9 (too wide for a 5 % claim; the clean-round medians are 0.957 and 0.970 in
  f32, 0.961 and 1.07 in bf16, on 5-9 clean rounds each). Inside the noise: no regression is shown and none
  could be resolved at this machine's noise. (The min-of-rounds column for these workloads, 0.48-1.7, is the
  noise itself: the control lane's own minima differ from the before lane's by the same factors.)
  One GEMM (committed table above, plain / production, medians of per-round ratios): exact f32 down 2.1x at 1
  row, 2.0x at 16, 1.4x at 64; o global 1.9x, 1.8x, 1.6x; o sliding 2.0x, 2.2x, 1.4x; ple_gate / ple_out 1.3x
  at each. (A second 16-row run in the same loaded window, nnsmall16_f32.txt, not committed: down 2.7x, o global
  3.1x, o sliding 2.2x, ple 1.85x. In the quieter minutes of development the stage profiler's gemm mode read
  down 97 us -> 35 us at 16 rows: 4.2 MiB in 35 us = 120 GB/s, about 55 % of the 220 GB/s copy ceiling, short of
  the 70 % the stage profile's estimate assumed, since the lane pays a reduce dispatch and a barrier per GEMM.)
  bf16 down 1.3x, 1.1x, 1.1x; o global 1.6x, 1.7x, 1.4x; o sliding 1.3x, 1.4x, 1.3x; ple 1.2x, 1.16x, 1.0x.
  qkv and gate|up are not routed and read 0.9-1.1x of themselves (production = the plain kernel there; the forced split measured 0.7-1.3x, a loss as
  often as a gain, which is why the gate stops at 64 tiles). bf16 down is the weakest lane: its 64x64 tile gives
  only 8 tiles x 8 partitions of four simdgroups, and under this load its rounds spread [0.78, 1.51].
  Predicted against measured: the stage profile's recommendation 3 estimated 7.3 -> 4.1 ms (1.8x) for 70 % of
  the copy ceiling on the four shapes. Measured 1.4-1.6x with 55 %. Inferred, not measured: that the rest is the
  per-GEMM reduce + barrier (a fused reduce, or the reduce folded into the following norm + residual kernel,
  would remove it) and the unchanged qkv / gate|up / PLE-projection stages.

VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNVERIFIED
  Verified: the lane's results against an f64 reference at M in {1, 7, 16, 63, 64} and the real K / N, through
  gemm, ExactF32.nn, gemm_bf16, Bf16.nn and the forced entries (tests/gemm_small_m.rs, gemm_small_m_bf16.rs;
  mutation-checked: dropping the A partition offset, or skipping one tile of one partition, fails 4 of 7 /
  3 of 6 tests with errors of 500-1000x the budget, and a poisoned scratch turns an unwritten element into a
  NaN); bit-for-bit repeats over 48 runs; the route per shape; the 160-case and 2500-case GEMM fuzz
  (gemm_fuzz_deep, seed 0xdeadbeef) unchanged and green with the lane in the sampled shapes; the encode
  speedups above and the per-GEMM ratios.
  Inferred: that the 64x32 and 8x1024 kernels are the same as df5d329's (from the gate's tests and the routing
  test, not from a dispatch trace of the benchmark's own forward); that the fuzz reaches the lane often
  (m, n <= ~190 and K to 2049 put many f32 NN cases under 64 tiles; not counted).
  Unverified: a stage profile of the new build (no quiet window); the lane on f16 and relaxed f32 (not routed);
  other consumers' (ojas, qd-metal, MLSystemsLab) bit-level expectations at small-M f32 NN shapes. The bits
  differ from df5d329's there, inside the bound above, and a row's bits now depend on M across the gate (M <= 96
  and M >= 97 at N 512 sum K in different orders; one dispatch computed a row the same at any M). rg over
  ojas, Lappi-decision, MLSystemsLab, sparsl found no golden that pins small-M GEMM bits, but qd-metal's
  tests/gpu.rs and ledger_rows.rs assert logits "bit identical" between two arms of a decision, which holds only
  while both arms take the same side of the gate at every GEMM; not run here.
  Not changed, and not a latency-bound shape: the NT per-layer-input projection (131-176 us, one dispatch per
  forward, 384 tiles).
  (Inferred, not tested: that one dispatch computed a row the same at any M.)
