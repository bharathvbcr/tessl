EmbeddingGemma 2 vision tower speed: before, after (ExactF32) and after (Bf16), paired and interleaved
=======================================================================================================
Machine: Apple M5 Pro, 2026-10-15. A GPU other sessions share: the load average read 66 to 130 and
other lanes' benchmarks ran on the same GPU during these rounds, so some rounds are contended (see
"Contention" below). Data: embedgemma2_vision_speed_m5pro.json (bench_paired's report: every
round of every lane, the summary, the lane argv, the env; bench_paired --out also keeps the lanes'
stdout and stderr). embedgemma2_vision_speed_m5pro_run1.json is an earlier 6-round run of the same
lanes at BENCH_ITERS=6, kept because it was worse (spreads 0.18 to 0.80) and the figures below do
not rest on it. Plot: embedgemma2_vision_speed_m5pro.svg (`plot_results vision-speed`; it also reads embedgemma2_vision_profile_m5pro.json).

What is timed: one EmbedGemma2VisionModel::encode_images call on the real checkpoint, host pixels
and positions to host soft tokens (pixel packing, upload, the forwards, readback); not the load.
Workloads: "1img" (image 0 of the goldens, 224 x 224, 2304 real patches, 2520 padded) and "8img"
(8 images cycling the two goldens, 2 forwards). src/bin/bench_embedgemma2_vision.rs.

Lanes (each round runs every lane once as a fresh process, in lane order on one round and
reversed on the next):
  before-433e659   the library at 433e659 (the merge base), built in its own worktree with its own
                   target dir; the bench source is f4a9ece's (the first version that prints HASH),
                   copied into that tree: only src/bin differs, so the lane times 433e659's tower
  after-f32        this branch, BENCH_PRECISION=f32 (GemmOperands::ExactF32)
  after-bf16       this branch, BENCH_PRECISION=bf16 (GemmOperands::Bf16)

Contention. Rounds 1 to 4 of the 1-image workload read 340 to 407 ms for the before lane in the
rounds where it should read about 165 to 180: another process was using the GPU, unevenly across
lanes, so the all-round medians above mix contended and clean rounds, and their spreads are
wide. Rounds 5 to 8 are clean for 1 image (the lanes' times agree round to round). Selecting
them is post hoc and not what the bound was fixed on; the all-round figures above are the
reported ones, and these are shown beside them:
                                  1img (rounds 5-8)                 8img (rounds 5-8)
  before-433e659   median ms       173.6                            1679.2
  after-f32        median ms       169.3  (0.974x of before; 0.960 to 0.992)   1531.9 (0.958x; 0.849 to 0.975)
  after-bf16       median ms        58.2  (0.349x of f32; 0.342 to 0.355)      542.9  (0.354x of f32; 0.348 to 0.366)
(The 8-image rounds are less steady than the 1-image ones even in rounds 5 to 8: the before
lane reads 1496 to 1694 ms.)
So: Bf16 is 2.8x to 2.9x faster than ExactF32 over the clean rounds (3.1x over all 8 rounds at 8
images, where its spread [0.260, 0.366] does not touch 1.0), and ExactF32 is about 3 to 4% faster
than before - consistent with the stage profile's rope saving (8.4 -> 3.1 ms of 183 ms at 1 image,
85.9 -> 24.8 ms of 1388 at 8) but at the edge of what this run resolves: the 8-image ExactF32
spread over all rounds [0.849, 1.286] crosses 1.0, so that one is NOT a result by itself.
