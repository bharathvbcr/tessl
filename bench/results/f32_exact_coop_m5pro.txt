# Exact-f32 NN with a register accumulator vs production — M5 Pro, 2026-09-30
#
# Question: does a cooperative-destination (register-accumulator) exact-f32
# NN beat production's exact f32 NN (32x32, one simdgroup, zero pre-pass,
# TILE_F32)? Qwen35's train_step runs every GEMM on that kernel, about 5.6 s
# of its 7.2 s step at T = 2048 on the 2B. `TESSL_GEMM_TUNE=1 cargo build
# --release --bins`, then `target/release/bench_gemm_tile_tune`; one run,
# GPU idle (confirmed with the peer session). Bar set before the run: >= 1.3x
# over 6.4 TFLOP/s.
#
# Finding: the bar is not met. Exact f32 is compute-bound at about
# 5.5-6.1 TFLOP/s whether or not the accumulator lives in registers: the
# variants gain 0-11% on most shapes and lose up to 21% on narrow ones. The
# exception is the 2B's MLP up/gate shape (2048 x 6144 x 2048), where
# production runs at 4.1 TFLOP/s against its 5.7 at 2048^3 and the variants
# reach 5.6-5.9 (1.37-1.45x). Every variant's output is bit-identical to
# production's (max_rel_err 0). Decision: no exact-f32 coop kernel in the
# general path; the MLP-up anomaly is open (see docs/qwen35.md, Not done).
#
