Method change attempt 1 -> 2: BENCH_ITERS 30 -> 100 and BENCH_WARMUP 10 -> 30 on tessl and torch alike. It did not help; the larger
shapes also went out of bound in attempt 2, when load rose.

Cause (INFERRED, not proven): the sub-0.3 ms shapes (512^3, 1024^3, 2048^3, 2048x768x768) are dominated by sync-per-iteration host
latency and per-process clock state (attempt 1 per-round times: tessl 512^3 0.191 ms cold in round 1 vs 0.112-0.146 later; torch
1024^3 one round 0.435 ms vs about 0.21 otherwise), and in attempt 2 concurrent CPU builds (clippy-driver) added contention, which also
pushed the large shapes out. Not tested: a quiet machine, a longer timed section per process, CPU pinning.

Verdict: no verdict at the 1.25x bound on this machine. The torch 2.13 figures in README stay the only bf16-vs-MPS evidence, and they
remain the imported MLSystemsLab measurements, not this tree's kernels. No shape is declared below or at parity.

A re-measure needs a machine where both cargo slots are held for the timed section (no concurrent builds) and where the A/A control
is within the bound first. The scripts are reusable as they stand.
