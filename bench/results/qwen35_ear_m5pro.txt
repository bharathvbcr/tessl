Metric (SLQ, IST-DASLab/SLQ, eval/run_ear.py -> compute_baseline_topk / compute_kl_and_ear in
src/metrics/evaluation.py, read 2026-10-23 through WebFetch, which paraphrases rather than quotes):
at each position take the REFERENCE's top-10 logits and indices; gather the candidate's logits at the
same indices; p = softmax(reference top-10), q = softmax(gathered candidate), both over the 10 entries
only; EAR = sum_i min(p_i, q_i); mean over positions. SLQ weights positions by a calibration mask; with
no mask each weight is 1, which is what is computed. Ties in the reference break to the lower index.

Findings
  (a) A bf16 reference cannot anchor the 0.99 bar. Exact fp32 scored against transformers' bf16 forward
      gets only 0.988111 (default) and 0.991600 (text); the transformers bf16 forward against fp32 gets
      0.988105 and 0.991597. Against transformers fp32, tessl F32 (0.999997 / 0.999998) and tessl Bf16
      (0.997648 / 0.998021) clear 0.99 on both prompts. The card's bf16-reference rows are kept above and
      are the ones to quote only with this caveat.
  (b) MLX affine 4-bit, group size 64 round-to-nearest, is lossy by this measure: EAR about 0.85 (default)
      and 0.87 (text) against fp32, minimum 0.47 and 0.31. tessl's Q4 forward reproduces mlx-lm's own Q4
      forward on the same weights and ids: mean EAR against fp32 differs by 2.0e-4 (default) and 1.9e-5
      (text), and tessl's Q4 against mlx-lm's f32-activation Q4 is 0.997335 / 0.997911. The loss is the
      4-bit format, not the tessl path. The test asserts that parity (EAR_Q4_PARITY_DELTA = 5e-3, fixed
      before the first run); it asserts no 0.99 bar, which Q4 does not reach.
  (c) Q8: NOT MEASURED. No Qwen3.5 model path runs with Q8 weights. `gemv_q8` (src/nn.rs:716) is a
      standalone group-affine int8 GEMV (i8 packed, f32 scales and zeros) that only tests and
      bench_nn_kernels call; the model's weight variants are dense and MLX Q4 only, and there is neither
      a Q8 loader nor a quantizer for model weights. Measuring a Q8 model needs a new loader, which was
      out of scope; no second grid format was added.

Scope and limits: one 2B model, two prompts (155 and 512 positions, no calibration mask), top-10, mean
and min only. Weights are the Base release. EAR against a reference is only as good as the reference.
